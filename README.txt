GTA V10.23.6 — All Vocab Hear My Voice Speech Feedback

Built from V10.23.4 and preserves the level-based speech feedback.

WHAT IS NEW:
- Speak & Check now saves your latest spoken attempt temporarily.
- After the app listens and scores you, you can tap: ▶ Hear My Voice.
- You can also tap: 🗑 Clear Recording.
- Try Again / Clear Score clears the level result and the temporary voice attempt so you can retry cleanly.

SPEECH FEEDBACK STILL USES TEACHER LEVELS:
- Level 0 — Not Recognized
- Level 1 — Wrong Word
- Level 2 — Needs Practice
- Level 3 — Understandable
- Level 4 — Strong

WHERE IT WORKS:
- Speak cards
- Vocabulary cards
- Smart Practice → Listen & Repeat
- Saved Phrase Builder phrases

PHRASE BUILDER SAVED PHRASES:
- Keeps the permanent Record My Voice / Play My Recording / Clear Recording controls.
- Also has Speak & Check with temporary Hear My Voice replay.

NOTES:
- Speech scoring depends on browser Greek speech recognition support.
- Voice replay depends on microphone permission and MediaRecorder support.
- Temporary speech-check recordings are not meant to be permanently saved.
